NATION STATE PROFILE
The United Republic of Vesperia (URV) functions as a highly strategic, rapidly expanding economic power, maintaining critical oversight of global shipping lanes and high-capacity deep-water ports. Geographically the nation is defined by severe topographical and socioeconomic contrast — hyper-wealthy automated urban hubs walled off from dense, rugged mountain ranges and primordial frontier forests.
CAPITAL METROPOLIS — NOVA KAELEN
Nova Kaelen operates as the sprawling national epicentre. Vertical neon-and-glass corporate towers loom directly over decaying brutalist concrete sectors. The metropolis remains a permanent crucible of high-end industrial espionage, syndicate-run corruption and violent underground dissent.
ACTIVE THREAT MATRIX — THE 2020s CRISIS
Vesperia is embroiled in an unacknowledged asymmetric shadow war against the Shattered Crown Initiative (SCI) — a heavily capitalized ultra-nationalist separatist insurgent faction.
Modern hybrid warfare: weaponized commercial drone swarms, infrastructure-wide cyber-disruption, ruthless urban guerrilla tactics. Rolling blackouts are used to mask movement between sectors.
By embedding within the high-density civilian populace of Nova Kaelen's slums, the insurgents render traditional deployment, heavy artillery and conventional airstrikes entirely unviable. With public panic rising, state command structures face structural collapse.
